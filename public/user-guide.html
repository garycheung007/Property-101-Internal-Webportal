Property 101 Group — Internal Reference

Welcome & Roles

The Property 101 staff portal is where you manage the full lifecycle of your body corporate and incorporated society portfolio — from meeting preparation through to statutory disclosures and insurance renewals.

Your Role Determines What You See

Admin
Full access to everything — all complexes, all settings, Admin Panel, delete and archive functions.
Manager
Full access to your own portfolio. Can view all complexes, generate documents, manage meetings. No Admin Panel access.
Support
Dashboard auto-filters to your assigned manager's portfolio. Can generate disclosures and documents. No Admin Panel.
Accounts
Access to On-Charge Invoices (including delete/restore), plus read access to complexes and reports.

Logging In

1
Enter your username Type your first name only (e.g. laura) — the portal automatically adds @prop101.co.nz. You can also type your full email address.
2
Enter your password Type your password and click Sign In. Contact an admin if you need your password reset.
3
You're in Support staff will see the Dashboard filtered to their assigned manager's portfolio automatically.
💡
TipIf you get a login error, check that Caps Lock is off. Contact Gary if the problem continues.

Dashboard

Admin Manager Support

The Dashboard is your daily starting point. It shows alerts and actions across your portfolio at a glance.

Understanding the Stat Cards

Three cards appear at the top: Upcoming Actions (things due soon), Critical Alerts (issues needing immediate attention), and AGMs Remaining this year. Click any card to jump to that section below.

Critical Alerts (Red Zone)

These are urgent items — expired insurance, missed deadlines, overdue actions. Each row has:

  • A checkbox to select it — tick multiple rows, then use "Snooze X selected" to snooze them all at once
  • A Snooze button (hover to reveal) — postpones the alert for 1–7 days with a reason
  • An Audit Trail button (hover to reveal) — view and add notes against this alert
  • Click the row itself to open that complex

Upcoming Actions (Yellow Zone)

Items that are due within the threshold window. Each row has a Log Details button and a Navigate to Complex button. Levy actions also have a Mark Done button.

Snoozing an Alert

1
Click SnoozeHover the alert row and click the Snooze button, or tick the checkbox and use the batch button.
2
Choose duration (1–7 days) and enter a reasonThe reason is required. The modal shows when the alert will return.
3
ConfirmThe alert moves to the Snoozed panel at the bottom of the dashboard until the snooze expires.

Manager Filter

Admins see a dropdown at the top to filter by any manager. Support staff see only their assigned manager's portfolio — this is set automatically on login.

Meeting Cards

Upcoming meetings across the portfolio appear below the alerts. Each card shows the BC name, meeting type, date, and the next required action (e.g. NOI Required, NOM Required). Click a card to go to that complex's Meetings tab.

Complexes

Admin Manager Support

The Complexes page lists all body corporates and incorporated societies in the portfolio. Click any row to open that complex.

Finding a Complex

  • Use the search box — searches by name, BC number, or address
  • Filter by Manager, Type (BC or IS), or Status (Active / Archived)
  • The list defaults to showing only Active complexes

Meeting Checklist

Admin Manager Support

Inside any complex, the Meetings tab is where you track meeting preparation progress from NOI through to post-meeting minutes.

Selecting a Meeting

The left sidebar lists all meetings. Click one to open it in the right panel. Click New Meeting to schedule a new one.

Key Deadlines Strip

Six dates appear automatically based on the meeting date and your system settings:

  • NOI Preferred Date and NOI Statutory Deadline — when to issue the Notice of Items
  • NOI Response Due — deadline for responses (editable inline; set the time and reminder days here)
  • NOM Preferred Date and NOM Statutory Deadline — when to issue the Notice of Meeting
  • Meeting Date — the meeting itself

Working Through the Checklist

1
Tick checklist items as you complete themThere are 4 stages: NOI, NOM, Prior to Meeting, and After Meeting. Each has its own set of tasks.
2
Issued dates auto-stampWhen all items in the NOI, NOM, or Minutes stage are ticked, the system records today as the issued date — no need to enter it separately.
3
Complete the Post-Meeting UpdateOnce all four stages reach 100%, a card appears asking for post-meeting details (levy amounts, fund balances, etc.). Fill it in and click Save.
⚠
ImportantThe Post-Meeting Update card must be saved to update the complex's financial records and levy schedule. Don't dismiss it without saving.

Meeting Calendar

Admin Manager Support

A monthly calendar view of all meetings across the portfolio. Colour-coded by type: AGM (pink), EGM (violet), SGM (orange), Committee (sky).

  • Use Key Dates toggle to show or hide NOI/NOM deadline markers on the calendar
  • Filter by Manager or Meeting Type using the header dropdowns
  • Click Today to jump back to the current month
  • Click any meeting chip to navigate directly to that complex
  • The Upcoming Meetings panel lists the next 8 meetings across the portfolio with a days-until countdown

Document Preparation

Admin Manager Support

Found under Document Preparation in the sidebar. Generates NOI cover letters, response forms, and other meeting documents as Word (.docx) files, auto-filled with the complex's data.

Generating a Document

1
Search for the propertyType the name or BC number in the search box and select it from the dropdown.
2
Select the meeting (optional)If the document relates to a specific meeting, choose it. Otherwise the complex's general AGM data is used.
3
Click PreviewAn A4 preview appears on the right showing the completed document with all merge fields filled in.
4
Click Word to downloadDownloads a .docx file ready to send. The correct template (Body Corporate or Inc Society) is used automatically based on the complex type.

Available Templates

  • NOI Cover Letter
  • Response Form
  • Notice of Delegation
  • AIG Association Liability Form
  • Debt Collection Flowchart (admin only)
  • Declaration Form — Financial Statement (Inc Society only)
  • Conflict Register (download as Word or PDF)
💡
TipIf the Word button is greyed out, the template hasn't been uploaded yet. Ask an admin to upload it via Admin Panel → Templates.

Disclosure & CPL

Admin Manager Support

Found under Disclosure & CPL in the sidebar. Generates the three statutory disclosure documents required for property transactions.

Document Types

TypeUsed For
S146Pre-Contract Disclosure Statement — issued before a sale agreement is signed
S147Pre-Settlement Disclosure Statement — issued before settlement
CPLCertificate of Projected Levy — used by lenders for mortgage purposes

Generating a Disclosure

1
Search and select the complexType the name or BC number in the search box.
2
Choose the document typeClick S146, S147, or CPL. The fields shown will change based on your selection.
3
Fill in the transaction detailsEnter the unit number, annual levy, owner name, and lawyer details as required. Fields that are not needed for your document type are hidden automatically.
4
Enable Invoice (if applicable)Toggle on the invoice to charge for the disclosure. Select a pricing tier or enter a custom amount.
5
Click Preview, then WordPreview the document first, then download the completed .docx file.
⚠
Duplicate WarningIf the same complex, document type, and unit already has an outstanding invoice, you'll see a warning. A popup will ask for a reason before you can proceed to download.

On-Charge Invoices

Admin Manager Support Accounts

Found under Financial in the sidebar. Tracks disclosure and CPL invoices issued to buyers and lawyers.

Invoice Status Flow

Invoices move through three states: Outstanding → Recovered → (Deleted). Deleted invoices can be restored.

Creating a Manual Invoice

1
Click Create InvoiceSelect the property, document type (S146, S147, CPL, or Other), and unit number.
2
Set the amountFor S146/S147/CPL, choose a pricing tier. For Other, enter a custom amount. GST is calculated automatically.
3
Click Create InvoiceThe invoice appears in the Outstanding table immediately.

Marking an Invoice as Recovered

Click the Recover action on an outstanding invoice. A confirmation modal shows the invoice details — click Yes, mark recovered to confirm. The invoice moves to the Recovered table.

💡
Accounts RoleOnly admins and accounts-role users can delete or restore invoices. All other users can view invoices and mark them as recovered.

Response Library

Admin Manager Support

A library of pre-written email responses for common owner and committee enquiries. Saves time and keeps replies consistent.

Finding and Copying a Response

1
Search or browse by categoryCategories: General, Insurance, Meetings, Levy Queries, Maintenance, Disclosure, Complaints, Other.
2
Click a card to open itThe full formatted response is shown in a modal.
3
Click Copy to ClipboardThe response copies as both formatted HTML and plain text — paste it directly into an email.
💡
Admin OnlyOnly admins can add, edit, or delete responses. If you need a new response added to the library, contact Gary.

Contractors

Admin Manager Support

A directory of all contractors — insurance brokers, valuers, compliance companies, and others — used across the portfolio.

  • Search by company name or contact person
  • Filter by category using the dropdown
  • Toggle Preferred Only to show preferred contractors
  • The Coverage Gaps panel shows complexes that have no Insurance Broker or Valuer assigned
  • Only admins can mark a contractor as Preferred, or add/delete contractors

Reports

Admin Manager

Reports Page

Shows portfolio summary charts and a full property snapshot table. Three CSV exports are available:

  • Meeting Workload CSV — meetings per manager (total, AGMs, committee)
  • Full Portfolio List CSV — all active complexes with 21 data fields
  • Manager Portfolio CSV — same as above, filtered to a specific manager
